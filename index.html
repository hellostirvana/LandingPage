The current experience

What is a Founding Table?

It is an early, founder-coordinated gathering for people who want to discover a culture, enjoy a shared meal, and have a better kind of conversation.

01

Small by design

Each table is planned for 6–8 guests, keeping the experience personal enough for everyone to connect.

02

Curated with care

Stirvana reviews requests and coordinates each early table personally. A request is not a confirmed reservation.

03

Flexible settings

Early gatherings may take place in neutral, private, or professional spaces selected for the specific table.

04

Clear before commitment

Expect about $50–$60 per person. No payment is collected with your seat request; details come first.

How it works
