The early-stage details

Questions about the Founding Table

Stirvana is beginning with small, founder-coordinated gatherings in South Florida. Here is what we know now—and what a seat request means.

What is a Stirvana Founding Table?+

It is a small, curated gathering built around a shared meal, cultural discovery, and real conversation. These first tables are coordinated personally so Stirvana can learn what guests and potential hosts value most.

Where are Founding Tables happening?+

The current focus is South Florida. The date, area, and setting for a specific table will be shared during personal follow-up—not assumed when you submit a request.

How many people will be at a table?+

The early format is designed for approximately 6–8 guests. That size is intentional: large enough for different perspectives, but small enough for everyone to take part in the conversation.

What will it cost, and do I pay now?+

The expected price is about $50–$60 per person. No payment is due or collected when you request a seat. You will receive the details for a relevant table before deciding whether to participate.

Does requesting a seat confirm my place?+

No. Requesting a seat does not confirm a reservation. It tells Stirvana that you are interested and helps shape the first tables. We will review requests and reach out personally when there is a relevant next step.

What kind of venue should I expect?+

Early tables may use neutral, private, or professional spaces, depending on the gathering. Stirvana will share the specific setting before you decide whether to attend.

Can I attend solo or with other people?+

Either. The seat request asks whether you hope to come solo or with others and, if applicable, your group size. Sharing that information helps us understand how to shape a balanced table.

What about allergies or dietary needs?+

Use the optional notes field to tell us about allergies, dietary needs, or accessibility considerations. We cannot promise every table will be suitable, but we will use your information when discussing a relevant gathering with you.

I might like to host. What does that mean right now?+

You can share a food tradition, cultural story, welcoming space, or gathering idea. Stirvana is speaking with a small number of early collaborators; the hosting form starts a conversation and does not enroll you in a formal program.

Curious enough to join the first tables?

Share your interests now. We’ll review your request and follow up personally when there is a relevant next step.
